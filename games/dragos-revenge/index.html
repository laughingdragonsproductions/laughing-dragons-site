How to Play

  1. Move Drago with arrow keys or the on-screen D-pad - one square at a time.
  2. Walk into a green block to push it. You can push a block into active chattering teeth if the tile behind them is empty.
  3. Chattering teeth move on their own timer and chase you in eight directions (including diagonals).
  4. Trap all teeth - surrounded ones wait until every set is trapped, then they all become collectable dragon tiles.
  5. When all teeth are sleeping, they become dragon tiles. Walk over them to score, or push a block onto a unreachable tile to crush it (no points).
  6. Clear the level when every dragon tile is collected or crushed.
  7. You have 3 lives. Lose a life if the teeth catch you (or the timer runs out in Easy/Hard).
  8. Easy gives slower teeth and extra time; Hard is classic speed; Zen has no timer.
