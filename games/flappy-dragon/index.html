How to Play

  1. Tap, click, or press Space / ↑ to flap upward.
  2. Fly through gaps between stone pillars — each gap scores one point.
  3. Hit a pillar, the ground, or the sky and the run ends.
  4. The game speeds up as your score climbs.
  5. Beat score milestones to unlock new dragon colors.
  6. Easy uses 15% lighter gravity and jump — great for slower reaction times. Hard is the standard challenge.
