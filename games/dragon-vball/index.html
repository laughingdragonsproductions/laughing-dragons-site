How to Play

  1. Move your dragon up and down on the left side of the beach court.
  2. Use mouse, touch, W/S, or the arrow keys to track the ball.
  3. Hit the beach ball back over the net — angle depends on where you strike it.
  4. Score when the ball passes the AI dragon on the right. First to 7 wins the match.
  5. Keep a rally going — after 8 paddle hits in a row the ball catches fire!
  6. Win matches to unlock new dragon colors.
  7. Easy gives the AI more error and a slower ball. Hard is the standard challenge.
